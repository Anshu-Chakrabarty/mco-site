Our story

Quality and quantity

Starting in 2008, The M Co. has become the one-stop shop for all types of mannequins and related accessories. Though we're only in our second decade, we have served some of the biggest names in the fashion industry.

They trust us because the merchandise we provide is of the highest quality. Our company is not just about the quantity of our offerings. It is also focused on their quality.

Today we sell and rent mannequins, dress forms, body forms, heads, legs, torsos, racks, hangers, mirrors and steam irons, and our studio is available on rent for product shoots.
