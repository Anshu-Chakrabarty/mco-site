Price codes & quotes

What is a price code?

It’s a unique reference (for example MCO-HDL-7K3Q) created when you submit a request. It records the products, quantities, buy/rent choice and dates you picked. Our team uses it to send you an exact, itemised quote, and you can mention it on any call or WhatsApp message.

Why don’t you show prices online?

Pricing depends on model, finish, quantity and, for rentals, duration and dates. A price code lets us quote your exact requirement instead of showing a generic price that may not apply.

How fast will I get my quote?

Usually the same working day. Sending your code on WhatsApp is the fastest way.

Can I see products before deciding?

Of course. Visit our studio at 238B, Acharya Jagadish Chandra Bose Rd, Bhowanipore, Kolkata 700020. Call ahead on +91 98317 66745 so we can have your shortlisted pieces ready.

Do you deliver?

We can arrange delivery and pickup for both purchases and rentals. Tell us your location in the request form and delivery will be included in your quote.

Products & sizes

Are different skin tones, finishes and sizes available?

Yes. Our mannequins come in a range of sizes, skin tones and styles, from realistic and abstract to headless, colourful/metallic, sports and children’s. Mention your preference and we’ll match it.

Are the listed measurements exact?

The sizes shown are standard ranges for each category. Exact dimensions vary by model, and your quote confirms the precise measurements of the pieces you choose.

Do you offer bulk pricing for racks and hangers?

Yes. Racks are available single or in bulk, and hangers in any quantity. Add the quantity to your request and the quote will reflect volume pricing.

Rental

What can I rent?

Mannequins, dress forms, body forms, heads, legs, torsos, racks, T-stands, mirrors, steam irons and more, plus our photo studio.

How is rental priced?

By product, quantity and duration. Share your dates and we’ll quote the exact amount for your rental period.

What can I use rentals for?

Product photoshoots, product launches, fit and product trials, fashion shows, exhibitions, pop-ups and events of any size.

Can I combine studio and mannequin rental?

Yes. Book the studio and pick mannequins and props from our range, all in one quote.
