Headless Mannequins

Available to buyAvailable to rent8 styles

Our best-selling retail mannequin. With no head, all the attention goes to the garment, which suits everything from ethnic wear to western. Sturdy fibreglass bodies with realistic proportions and detachable arms make dressing quick.

Headless mannequins are the most practical choice for stores that change displays often. They fit any brand style, work with sarees, lehengas, gowns and western wear alike, and are easy to dress and move. Available in female and male bodies, standing and seated poses, and matte, gloss, black or skin-tone finishes.
