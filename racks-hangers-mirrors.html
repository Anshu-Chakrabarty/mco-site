Are the listed measurements exact?

The sizes shown are standard ranges for each category. Exact dimensions vary by model, and your quote confirms the precise measurements of the pieces you choose.

Do you offer bulk pricing for racks and hangers?

Yes. Racks are available single or in bulk, and hangers in any quantity. Add the quantity to your request and the quote will reflect volume pricing.

What is a price code?

It’s a unique reference (for example MCO-HDL-7K3Q) created when you submit a request. It records the products, quantities, buy/rent choice and dates you picked. Our team uses it to send you an exact, itemised quote, and you can mention it on any call or WhatsApp message.

Why don’t you show prices online?

Pricing depends on model, finish, quantity and, for rentals, duration and dates. A price code lets us quote your exact requirement instead of showing a generic price that may not apply.
